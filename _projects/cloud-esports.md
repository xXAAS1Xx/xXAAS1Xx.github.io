## Overview
Academic Project Lead (Feb 2026 – Present) in partnership with the **San Diego County Office of Education (SDCOE)**: Danny Passawonge, Chris Garcia, and Micheal Bruder.

## Key Contributions
* **Network Troubleshooting:** Partnered with school leaders to investigate and troubleshoot Nintendo Switch connectivity on a K-12 network, examining NAT types, firewall configurations, port forwarding, and DMZs.
* **Infrastructure Modeling:** Modeled multi-region cloud infrastructure for competitive esports to minimize packet latency and optimize routing.
* **Zero-Trust Security:** Configured network topology diagrams integrating zero-trust access controls and automated failover protocols.
* **Conference Presentation:** Presenting an interactive session at the CITE Annual Conference (Anaheim, Nov 17–19, 2026) on balancing access, security, and implementation for IT leaders and network administrators.
